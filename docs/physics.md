# Physics

The crashcat world, the bird's solid, shove, room, push, floor and bounds (`src/world/physics.js`).
The props are in `props.md`.

- Engine: crashcat (pure JS, based on Jolt). Only `src/world/physics.js` imports it, so the engine can change in one file. `registerShapes()` and `registerConstraints()` list only what is used, for tree shaking. Add a shape there before you use it.
- Units: world units (the ibis is about 2 tall), kg, `GRAVITY = 22`. The step is at most 1/120 s, as in `Character`.
- The controller still owns the root. `character.collide` (a hook after each controller sub step) runs the kcc: it sweeps the bird's solid (`BIRD.solid`, one upright capsule from just above `GROUND.stepUp` to the chest, so it walks up low steps and a wall stops it) and moves the root back out of props. The solid must be one convex shape: in crashcat 0.0.5 the kcc's padding correction does not handle a compound and throws (`setSupport is not a function`).
- Layers: `fixed` (ground), `fence` (stops light props only), `terrain` (static steps and ramps from `addTerrain()`: convex hulls; the bird walks on them, the props rest on them), `heavy` (blocks the bird: the bin), `light` (the feet and head kick it: the ball), `limb` (the bird's kinematic foot and head spheres, from the rig), `bird` (each bird's solid, the kcc inner body: it collides with nothing in the simulation, but `birdFilter` lets the sweep of one bird stop at the solid of another; the kcc skips its own inner body; the shove, room and ground rays skip this layer), `ghost` (a held prop; it collides with nothing), `flier` (the kinematic sphere of a flying miner, `BIRD.flier`, round `Flier.centre()`: it knocks light props; the bird sweep, the shove and room rays and the ground rays skip it). Use `filter.create()` and set `enabledObjectLayers[i] = 0`: `filter.createEmpty()` has a zero collision mask, and `disableObjectLayer()` also turns off the shared broadphase layer.
- Shove: rays ahead of the head find a heavy prop and set `controller.shove`. The animator puts the head down (the `brace` spring), so the forehead takes the weight and the beak points down. The head sphere in `BIRD.head` sits where the brace puts the head. Change both. The brace distance grows with speed (`BIRD.braceAhead`), so a run braces as a walk does. The rays must reach past the bill in the run pose (`look.ahead`), or the room check stops the bird first.
- Room: each sub step, `makeRoom()` casts rays to the posed head and bill (`BIRD.reach`, from `skullBill()` in `model.glsl`). The bird steps back by the overlap, and the prop gets a push. This keeps the beak out of the bin in actions (peck, threat).
- Push: whatever stops the bird (the forehead from `shove()`, or the bill from `makeRoom()`) takes the push in `push()`, always at forehead height (a low push slides the bin and does not tip it). Every bird push goes through `pushUpTo()`: it stops where the contact point moves at the bird's speed (`BIRD.bounce` times that for the first bump), with the effective mass at the point, turn included. Without this cap a bump threw the bin like it had no weight. Both checks run every sub step. There is no brace and no push in the air: the bird has nothing to push from, and it must be able to land on a prop.
- Push height on a short prop (a box): the bump goes no higher than `BIRD.pushAt[0]` (0.9) of its height (from its AABB), the steady push no higher than `pushAt[1]` (0.6). So a walk slides a box and a run knocks it over. A push near the top of a short box tipped it at a walk. The upright bin is taller, so it keeps the forehead height.
- Push tuning: `BIRD.shove` (steady push for each unit of speed: a walk stays below the tip force of the bin, a run is above it) and `BIRD.bump` (one impulse when the forehead first touches: the walk rock).
- Ground: `addGround(profile)` makes one static convex hull for each straight part of the profile, on the `fixed` layer, down to `GROUND_DEPTH`. With no profile, it is flat at 0. The ground rays hit it, so a ray must reach `GROUND_DEPTH` below 0. Only a dynamic prop gets `BIRD.clear`.
- Floor: `feetGround()` casts rays (`groundAt()`) down from `GROUND.stepUp` above each foot and toe tip (`GROUND.toes`) onto terrain and heavy props. It sets `controller.feet` (height plus `BIRD.clear`, and the surface normal) and `controller.floor` (the highest). One ray under the root lets the toes sink into a sloped top. The controller lands on the floor and falls (event `fall`, no flutter) when the bird walks off the edge. The pose lifts the bird by `position.y`. Footprints are skipped where the foot's ground is more than 0.05 above the street (`fx.height`). The ground rays do not treat a convex shape as solid: a ray that starts inside a wall goes through it, so a top above `stepUp` is not a floor.
- Jump: the flutter (`JUMP.flutter`, `JUMP.flutterRise`) reaches about 2.0, enough for the top of a fallen bin (1.5 to 1.7).
- Demo bounds: `controller.bounds` is a world box `{ x, z }`. In free play it is `PLAY` in `street.js`: the street from one footpath to the other, road included, and 0.5 onto the yard grass past each footpath, 96 long. If you make it longer, also move the backdrop `RINGS` out (about 38 past the ends), and grow `TUFTS.area` (with `tries`) and `GROUND_HALF` in `physics.js`. The fence for light props is 0.5 outside it.
- Birds: the solids of two birds stop 0.92 apart (two radii and the kcc padding). The bill and the legs are not in the solid, so a bill can still go into the other bird.
- Bird push: `nudgeBirds()` gives the other bird `BIRD.nudge.share` (0.6) of the part of a sub step's move past touching (`BIRD.nudge.touch`), along the line between the two. The other bird takes it at its next sub step (`takeNudge()`), before its own sweep, so a wall or a prop still stops it, and the bounds still hold. So a run into the side of a walking bird pushes it out of the way (about 0.8 in a test) while it walks on, and a still bird slows the bird that pushes it to about the share of its speed. A direct move of the root, not a force: the controller owns the root. The pushed bird has no reaction pose.
- A root move longer than `BIRD.teleport` in one sub step jumps, with no sweep: a long kcc sweep can stall the page.
- A sleeping body ignores a new hinge target. Wake it with an impulse if you drive the lid from code.
- The ground body needs its own friction. crashcat combines the two values, and the default ground friction lets the bin slide where it must tip.
