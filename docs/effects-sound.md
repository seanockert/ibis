# Events, sound and effects

Character events, `src/ibis/sound.js` and `src/ibis/effects.js`.

- Events (`character.events`): `crouch`, `jump`, `land` (`impact`), `liftOff`, `step` (`foot`, `scuff`), `flap` (`amount`, `time`), and action events (`thump` with `dust`, `shakeOff`, `grab` and `drop` for `physics.js`, and for sound only `call`, `hiss`, `clack`, `stamp`). `Physics.grab()` adds `pick` (`hold`) when the bill takes a prop: a soft snap in `sound.js`. The demo uses `shakeOff` to take half the wet and half the mud off the bird, and `fx.shakeOff()` throws drops and mud blobs.
- `flap`: `beatEvents()` in the animator reads the final pose, so every wing beat (air pose, actions, flicks) gives one. It fires as the wings start down. `amount` is the height of the beat, `time` the seconds of the up stroke. Beats below `BEAT.min` (0.3) give no event, so the run flap and the walk swing are silent.
- Sound (`sound.js`): one `AudioContext`, started by a key or tap. On iOS only the end of a touch (`pointerup`, `touchend`) can start it, not `pointerdown`. iOS also mutes Web Audio when the ring/silent switch is on silent. Before that and while muted, the context is suspended: no sound plays or queues, and the audio thread does no work. Each sound is a voice (gain from the distance to the camera, `SOUND.near`; pan from the camera's x) with noise bursts and tones. A step is a soft, padded pat (low-pass noise, slow attack, no click). Wet or muddy feet add a squelch to each step, and a puddle splash has its own squelch. The honk sample plays at `pitch` times a random ±6%; `hash()` gives the variation. `SOUND.maxVoices` caps sounds at one time, and a compressor stops clipping. The demo makes sound only in the frame loop (not in fixed views or the bench); `sound=0` turns it off (there is no mute button now; `sound.muted` still works).
- Noisy miner calls: a flier's `call` event (`kind` `chip` or `scold`, `at` world) goes from `Level.update()` to `sound.minerCall()`. `chip` (`public/sfx/miner-chip.m4a`, 0.6 s, four alarm chips) is the alert on a perch. `scold` (`public/sfx/miner-scold.m4a`, 0.8 s, three harsh notes) is the attack. Both are cut from a field recording of a noisy miner, raised to a peak of -2 dB, and encoded as the honk. They play through a lowpass (`SOUND.minerTone`), with a wide pitch change (`minerPitch`), and a call within `SOUND.minerGap` of the last miner call is skipped, so two miners do not call over each other. The call rates are `SENTRY.chip` and `SENTRY.scold`. `load(name, url)` loads each recording by name, in `main.js`.
- A hurt miner (`flight.hurt()`) calls `yelp`: the chip recording at `SOUND.yelpPitch`, level `yelp`. A yelp skips the `minerGap` test. `Level.update()` draws hit lines on the miner too (`fx.hitLines(flier)`), at the miner's scale times `HURT.lines`, held `HURT.hold` seconds (`fx.hit(ibis, size, hold)`), and `fx.impact(at)`: `IMPACT.count` puffs that burst out all round the point.
- Volumes: `SOUND` at the top of `sound.js`: `volume` for all sounds, and `levels` for each sound (1 as made, 0 off). Tune there, not in the sound functions.
- To add a sound: add an event to the action (`events`), then a case in `IbisSound.update()`, and a level in `SOUND.levels`. To change the honk, trim the silence, encode mono AAC at 32 kbps (`afconvert -f m4af -d aac -b 32000 in.wav out.m4a`), and keep it below about 10 KB. Use AAC, not MP3: MP3 adds silence at the start.
- Effects (`effects.js`): run dust above `RUN_DUST_SPEED`: one line of puffs on the ground behind the root, one each `RUN_DUST.every` of distance (not one for each foot), skid dust, a ring on a hard `land`, a burst for events with `dust: 'rump'` or `'feet'`. Footprints at each `step` and `land` (on flat street only: not on a slope, a step or a prop; at `fx.height(x, z)`), as wide as the SDF toes, along the rig's toes and foot turn, held 2.5 s then faded over 3.5 s. The colour comes from the surface and the feet (`printStyle()`): `fx.surface(x, z)` (set by the demo to `printOn()` in `street.js` with `env`) gives dry and wet colours for each strip (pressed grass, dust on the footpath, light dust on the road, dark when wet); mud wins over wet, wet over dry. With no surface: dust, as before. Each print has a lit and a shade colour and takes the sun's shadow. They write depth so overlapping parts blend once. Anger lines show while `animator.anger` (from the actions' `anger` field) is above 0.3. Hit lines (`fx.hit(ibis)`, from a miner's dive that hits): five longer strokes in an arc over the head (`HIT`), in the style of the anger lines, that burst out with an overshoot, shake hard and shrink away after `HIT.hold`. They use the mark ink, so the backdrop trees don't cover them. Alert marks (`alertMarks()`): an ink `?` or `!` over the head of a flier with `mark` set, turned to the camera, `MARK.size` high at a fixed world size (not the bird's scale). They pop in as the anger lines do and rock: a `?` more as `flight.agitation` grows. A new mark pops in again. They use their own ink material with `transparent: true` and `renderOrder` 5: the backdrop trees are transparent, so they draw after all opaque meshes and covered an opaque mark. Puffs keep growing and dissolve (outline thins, then noise holes), so they stay opaque: no transparency or sorting. Each puff takes `ibis.mud` when made (`aMud`) and blends to mud-brown dust.
