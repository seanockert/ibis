# Land

Ground that is not straight along x: hills, gullies, bumps, dirt and mud (`src/world/land.js`, a `land` area in the level JSON).
The level format is in `levels.md`.

## How it works

- A `land` area owns all the ground at z from `box.z[0]` out to the near side (+z), across all x. The street ends there: `joinGrounds()` in `load.js` cuts the street's profile (the street mesh and the physics slabs) at `ground.cut`. The park starts at the near fence line (7.1), so the seam is under the fence and the gate.
- `Field`: the heights from `features`, on a grid `LAND.cell` (1) apart over `box`, made once at load. The mesh, the physics (`physics.addMesh()`, one triangle mesh) and `height()` use the same triangles (the diagonal of each cell from (i + 1, j) to (i, j + 1)), so the feet, the props, the tufts and the prints sit on what is drawn. The heights fade to 0 within `LAND.edge` (4) of the box edge, so the land meets the street and the flat land round the box. Keep the box edges whole cells from `box.x[0]` and `box.z[0]`.
- Lowest height: `LAND.low` (-0.8), and the floor in mud `LAND.sink` (0.28) below that. The physics ground rays reach `GROUND_DEPTH` (1.5) below 0; a ray that misses gives 0, and the bird stood on air over the deepest mud when it was 1. A triangle mesh has no inside.
- Features: `hill` (`at`, `radius` [rx, rz], `height`, `turn`: (1 - r²)², flat on top, steepest at about 0.58 of the radius: slope about 1.54 × height / radius), `gully`, `noise` (`scale`, `height`), and the wear features below.
- Pool (`at`, `radius` (a number or [rx, rz]), `heading`, `depth` 0.4, `mud` 0.75 of the radius, `bank` 0.8): an oval mud hole with a ragged edge (`poolEdge()`, warped by noise), as the gully's mud. Its mud fades over about 1 unit: over less, the 0.5-unit texels showed as straight facets. It replaced the street's `Puddle` mesh.
- Gully (`path` through a Catmull-Rom curve, `width`, `vary`, `depth`, `mud`, `dry`, `bank`, `taper`): a noise along the curve sets the half width (± `vary`), the depth (deeper where wider) and the wetness (none on about `dry` of the length), at each curve point (`prepare()`), so it widens into pools, narrows and dries out. `mud`: the part of the half width that is mud where wet. `bank`: the trampled band past the mud. `hill.turn`: radians. It narrows to nothing over `taper` (10) at each end, so it peters out in the park, not at the box edge. A 2D noise wobbles the distance, so the edges are not smooth. The noise along the curve must change in a few units: at 0.05 per unit (four noise cells over the gully) most of it was dry.
- Wear (the dirt): one smooth value, 0 grass to 1 bare, from rules for where grass dies: `track` (a desire line: `path`, `width`, `vary`, `wear`), `dirt` (a worn patch: `at`, `radius`, `wear`), `shade` (thin grass under each tree: `areas.js` adds one for each gum and bottlebrush in the box, `SHADE`), the gully banks (more where there is mud, broken by noise), and `patches` (weak wear where a noise is high). They join as 1 - (1 - a)(1 - b). Round shapes are warped by noise (`warp()`). The shader adds two noises (`LAND.ragged`) and cuts two steps: worn, thin grass (`LAND.worn`, the `worn` colour over the grass) and bare dirt (`LAND.dirt`). So the edges are ragged, grass islands stay in the dirt, and grass sprigs (the marks) stay on it. Hard round patches with a dark rim read as decals; much fine noise read as a rash of specks.
- Slopes: keep them gentle. The park hill (4.6 high, radius 26 by 22) is about 16 degrees at its steepest; the bird walks and runs up it and its floor stays within 0.17 of the ground.
- Surface: wear in r, mud in g of a texture, `LAND.surface` (0.5) a texel over the box; both fade at the box edge. The shader reads it once. `kind(x, z)` on the CPU (`grass`, `dirt`, `mud`) uses the same values without the noise: the footprints (`LAND_PRINTS`), the step sound (`under`) and the tufts use it.
- Mud: the model in `mud.js`: its colours (`mudUniforms()`), its water (`MUD_WATER`: a shallow band, slow streaks of sky, rings) where the mud (with the noise) is over `WATER.at`, a dark line at the water edge and a fainter one round the mud, and its rings (`Ripples`). A step or a landing on `mud` is a puddle splash (`level.js`): mud on the bird (`splashMud()`), mud blobs (`fx.splash()`), the splash sound, and a ring (`Land.splash()`). - Deep mud: the physics floor (`Field.floor()`, `floors`) is up to `LAND.sink` below the drawn mud, so the feet sink. The mud's depth bias is 0 (`aMud` on each vertex), so it hides them; elsewhere the bias stays, for the outline round the feet. Without physics the bird stands on `ground.floor`.
- Mesh: the box in `LAND.cell` cells, and flat cells round it that grow out to `LAND.reach` (450), as the street's. No rows before the box in z. About 27.6k triangles for the park, one draw, `renderOrder` -1 and the street's depth bias.
- Shader: the street's grass (`GRASS`, `GRASS_MARKS` from `street.js`), so the two match at the seam. Dirt with a darker rim; mud wetter in the middle with soft pale sheen. Grass near the mud is damp (darker). Slopes: a soft ramp on N.L less the N.L of flat ground (`LAND.slope`): warmer toward the sun, cooler away. The street's hard form tone (N.L below 0.75) made a stain on the lee side of the hill.
- Tufts: `wildTufts()`: clusters on the grass as the street's, and long grass along each gully from the mud edge (or the middle, where dry) out past the bank, taller next to the mud. Not where the wear is over `WILD.bare` or in the mud. Its own `Tufts` (one more draw). The street's tufts stop at the seam (`keep`).
- Wet ground: the land shader draws the sprinkler's wet patch (`GROUND_WET`) as the street's does.
- Plants: `rows` in the area (`plantRows()` in `areas.js`): one plant every `every` units along a line, `jitter`, a kind from `kinds` (a name, or plant options) by `seed`. Rows skip mud and low ground. Rows and plants outside the area's `bounds` get no solid: the bounds stop the bird before them. The park has rows of shrubs and bottlebrushes on three sides, gums behind them, no fence, and a gum on the hill top with the football by it. The clover and the sprinkler (its tap inside the gate) are in the park.
- A land area can be the only ground (`meadow.json`): `Land` gets `base`, its flat cells go all round the box, and `profile` is `[]` (no street slabs). `Field` binds `height`, `floor`, `kind`, `print`: the level passes them as functions.
- Without physics (`props=0`) the bird follows `ground.height` (`groundOf()`, `floorOf()` in `level.js`).
- The camera stays `CAM_MIN_Y` above the ground under it (`Stage.keepAbove(height)`).
- The backdrop rings are `out` past the level bounds (`RINGS` in `backdrop.js`), so they go round the park too.

## Cost

- GPU, 1200x800, ratio 1.5, `npm run gpu`: the street start 7.5 ms (7.5 before the park); a view in the park inside the gate 8.1 ms (7.75 before the wear, the mud water, and the sprinkler and clover moved there, which are in that view).
- Load: building the park's `Field` takes about 27 ms (dev server; 92 ms before the indexes). A curve keeps a grid of the segments near each 4-unit cell (`CURVE_CELL`), and the field a grid of the wear features that reach each cell (`WEAR_CELL`): a sample tests only those. The grid points need only the mud (`mudAt()`). With one loop over all features for each sample, the 20 shade features took 38 ms.
- Feature types are a table (`FEATURES` in `land.js`): `height(ft, x, z)` and/or `wear(ft, x, z, out)`. A new type is an entry there, and `prepare()` if it needs sizes ready (`bounds` lets the wear grid skip it far away).

## Not done

- Footprints are skipped where the ground normal y is below 0.98 (`footprint()` in `effects.js`): the steeper sides of the hill get none.
- Frogs at the gully at night.
